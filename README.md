## 📊 **Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard**

### Overview
This repository contains a Power BI dashboard for analyzing customer churn in a telecommunications company. The dashboard provides insights into why customers are leaving and helps identify key factors influencing churn rates.

---

### 📖 **Dataset**
The analysis is based on the `Telco_customer_churn.xlsx` dataset, which includes details such as:
- Customer demographics (e.g., gender, age)
- Service details (e.g., internet service type, contract length)
- Financial metrics (e.g., monthly charges, total revenue)
- Churn status and reasons

#### Key Columns Used:
- **Customer Status**: Indicates whether a customer has churned or stayed.
- **Churn Reason**: Reasons for customer churn (if applicable).
- **Internet Service**: Type of internet service used (DSL, Fiber Optic, No).
- **Contract**: Contract type (Month-to-month, One year, Two year).
- **Payment Method**: Payment method used (Electronic check, Mailed check, Bank transfer, Credit card).
- **Monthly Charge**: Monthly bill amount.
- **Total Revenue**: Total revenue generated by the customer.
- **Tenure**: Number of months the customer has been with the company.

---

### 🎨 **Dashboard Highlights**
The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of customer churn through various visualizations:

1. **KPI Cards:**
   - **Total Customers**: Overall number of customers in the dataset.
   - **Churned Customers**: Number of customers who have left.
   - **Churn Rate**: Percentage of customers who have churned.
   - **Total Revenue**: Total revenue generated by all customers.

2. **Churn Rate Breakdowns:**
   - **Churn Rate by Payment Method**: Shows how different payment methods affect churn.
   - **Churn Rate by Internet Service**: Compares churn rates across different internet services.
   - **Churn Rate by Contract**: Analyzes churn rates for different contract types.
   - **Churn Rate by Device Protection**: Examines the impact of device protection on churn.
   - **Churn Rate by Contract and Multiple Lines**: Combines contract type and multiple lines usage to identify patterns.

3. **Additional Metrics:**
   - **Average Monthly Revenue Per User**
   - **Average Churn Score**
   - **Average CLTV (Customer Lifetime Value)**
   - **Average Contract Length**
   - **Average Monthly Charge**
   - **Average Tenure of Churned Customers**
   - **Average Tenure**
   - **Average Monthly Charge of Churned Customers**

### 🤔 **Key Insights**

1. **Churn Rate by Contract:**
   - Customers on month-to-month contracts have the highest churn rate.
   - Customers on two-year contracts have the lowest churn rate.

2. **Churn Rate by Internet Service:**
   - Customers using DSL have a higher churn rate compared to Fiber Optic users.

3. **Payment Method Impact:**
   - Customers paying via electronic checks have a lower churn rate compared to mailed checks or bank transfers.

4. **Device Protection:**
   - Customers with device protection tend to have lower churn rates.

5. **Revenue and Tenure:**
   - Churned customers have a shorter average tenure and slightly higher average monthly charges.
